Failure Mode 02

Why NOT standard GIS?

2D

A heatmap shows pollution on the ground. Your argument is about vertical breach — pollution rising 30 ft to hit the 3rd floor. GIS cannot show height of the plume relative to the building facade.

Static

GIS captures the crime scene. The Multi-Layer model captures the crime being committed — the rate of accumulation as speed drops.

Multi-Layer Solution

Verticality + Time = arguments GIS can never make. The simulation lives in 3D and in motion.
